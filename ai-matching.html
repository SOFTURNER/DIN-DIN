🎯 The Problem

Impact founders like Alex (biological water treatment) and Maria (earthquake prediction AI) have brilliant solutions but struggle to find short-term business cases and funding for pilots. The core challenge: connecting with companies willing to pilot new technologies.

💡 Status Quo: It's hard for impact founders to reach pilot-ready companies, and difficult to build a business case beyond the product's core purpose that secures short-term funding.

✨ Building a Winning Business Case

Instead of waiting for companies to need your core product, we help startups identify immediate value propositions that solve corporate pain points:

💰 Profitability Pathways

Revenue Generation: License the technology for $X/month, generating recurring revenue from day 1

Cost Savings: Help corporates reduce operational costs (labor, energy, waste) - share 20-30% of annual savings

Efficiency Gains: Increase corporate productivity by Y%, negotiate a % of productivity gains as revenue

Sustainability Credits: Generate carbon credits or regulatory compliance credits worth $Z - split revenue

🤝 How Pilots Become Profitable

Phase 1 (Months 1-3): Pilot deployment - startup provides tech, corporate tests with real data

Phase 2 (Months 4-6): Proof of value - measure ROI, cost savings, or efficiency gains

Phase 3 (Months 7+): Commercial scaling - startup generates $50K-$500K+ annual recurring revenue (ARR)

📊 Real Example: Water treatment startup reduces corporate water costs by $100K/year → Split 25% = $25K/year pilot revenue. Scale to 10 corporates = $250K ARR within 18 months.

🎯 What Makes a Corporate Pilot-Ready

  • Has a measurable pain point (cost, efficiency, compliance)
  • Budget to test ($10K-$100K pilot investment)
  • Clear KPIs (cost savings %, time reduction %, compliance improvement)
  • Timeline for ROI evaluation (typically 6 months)
  • Willingness to scale if pilot succeeds

💡 Our AI Does the Matching

Our system identifies:

  1. Startup capabilities: What your tech can actually do
  2. Corporate pain points: Where they lose money or waste time
  3. Hidden use cases: Ways your tech solves problems beyond its original purpose
  4. Financial fit: Can the pilot ROI justify a long-term contract?
  5. Decision makers: Who in the corporate has budget and urgency?
